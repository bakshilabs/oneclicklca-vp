Every buyer has an alternative, including doing nothing

How I would map a buyer's best alternatives. These are hypotheses to test in win/loss interviews.

01

Wait and do nothing

Why buyers choose it
No mandate yet and other priorities.
What it costs them
Exposure as requirements tighten, and bids lost where carbon data is asked for.

02

Spreadsheets and in-house tools

Why buyers choose it
Familiar and needs no new budget.
What it costs them
Manual effort, hard-to-audit results and no scale across projects.

03

Consultants

Why buyers choose it
Expertise on demand.
What it costs them
Cost per study, slower turnaround and knowledge that leaves with the consultant.

04

Single-purpose tools

Why buyers choose it
Cheap for one task or one standard.
What it costs them
Gaps across standards and workflows, and data entered twice.

Why buyers choose it
Incumbency or a feature comparison.
